The best gifts for dad are the things he would never buy for himself. A watch he has admired for years. A leather piece that replaces something worn thin. A pair of sneakers that look sharper than the ones he wears to the market. Fathers are famously hard to shop for, so the best gifts for dad tend to be quiet, well made and used every day.

This list has 20 of the best gifts for dad and for a father in law, grouped by category: watches, leather goods and sneakers. Many are pieces from the Vellora edit, linked so you can see the exact item. Others are simple styles to look for. After the list, we cover men who have everything, 50th birthdays, fathers in law and Diwali. If you need one rule, the best gifts for dad fit the way he already lives and simply do it a little better.

How to choose a gift he will actually use

Look before you shop. What is on his wrist? What does he carry when he leaves the house? Which shoes does he reach for on a Sunday? The answers point you to the right category faster than any gift guide.

  • A man who wears a watch daily will notice a better one.
  • A man who carries papers, a phone and reading glasses will use a good bag.
  • A man who walks every morning will wear good sneakers until they fall apart.

Then match the tone. Some fathers like formal things: gold, polished leather, a crisp shirt. Others are practical and want steel, rubber soles and no fuss. Neither is better. The gift should suit him, not the occasion.

Watches

A watch is the most traditional gift for a father, and for good reason. He wears it every day, and it often gets passed down. Our watch edit is small and considered.

1. A yellow gold day and date watch

The Day-Date 40, reference 228238 is a 40 mm yellow gold watch on an Oyster case. A day-date display shows the weekday as well as the date. For a father who dresses formally and appreciates fine watches, it is a gift meant to be kept.

Day-Date 40 reference 228238 in yellow gold

2. A slim gold dress watch

The 1908, reference 52508, is 39 mm in 18 ct yellow gold with a polished finish. It is quieter than a sports watch and sits neatly under a shirt cuff. It suits a man who wears a watch to weddings, pujas and dinners.

3. A steel GMT watch for the traveler

The GMT-Master II, reference 126710GRNR is a 40 mm watch in Oystersteel. A GMT complication adds a 24-hour hand so he can follow a second time zone. It is ideal for a father with children abroad who likes to know what time it is where they are.

4. A solar chronograph

The Seiko Prospex Speedtimer Go Large Solar, SSC915P1 is a sporty chronograph that charges from light. Solar watches rarely ask for attention, which suits a father who does not want another thing to maintain.

Seiko Prospex Speedtimer Go Large Solar chronograph, reference SSC915P1

5. A solar watch with a racing theme

The Seiko Prospex Circuit Race Solar, SSC941P1, suits a dad who never misses a motor race on a Sunday afternoon. Sporty, legible and easy to live with.

6. A new strap for a watch he already loves

If he already has a watch he will not give up, a new strap can make it feel fresh. Leather for formal wear, rubber or fabric for weekends. Check the lug width on his watch before you buy. This is a style suggestion, not part of our edit.

Leather goods

Leather is the other classic category for fathers. It wears in rather than out, and good leather looks better after a few years of use. Apart from idea 7, these are styles to look for rather than pieces we carry.

7. A trunk-shaped shoulder bag

The LV x TM Side Trunk MM has a compact, structured trunk shape. It suits a stylish father who wants something more interesting than a plain messenger bag.

8. A leather watch roll

A roll that holds two or three watches protects them when he travels. It is a thoughtful pairing with any watch on this list.

9. A slim bifold wallet

If you are reading up on the best wallets for men 2026 has brought out, the style to look for is a slim bifold in plain, full-grain leather with only a few card slots. Wallets sit outside our current edit, so treat this as a style note.

10. A leather card holder

Many men now carry fewer cards. A slim card holder fits a front pocket and suits a father who has moved most payments to his phone.

11. A plain leather belt

A belt in black or dark brown with a simple buckle is worn almost every day. Reversible styles give him two colors in one.

12. A leather document folio

For a father who still handles property papers, bank documents or his own business files, a structured folio keeps everything together.

13. A leather weekender

A soft leather bag sized for two or three nights suits a father who travels to visit family. Look for sturdy handles and a shoulder strap.

14. A leather valet tray

A small tray on his dresser for keys, watch and coins. It is a quiet gift that he will see every morning and every night.

Best gifts for men who have everything

The hardest fathers to shop for already own the basics. The best gifts for men who have everything are upgrades, not additions. Look for the thing he uses most and find a finer version of it.

  • If he wears an old steel watch every day, a GMT or a solar chronograph is a clear step up.
  • If his favorite sneakers are falling apart, a better pair in the same color is a gift he will wear immediately.
  • If he has a watch collection, a watch box or roll respects what he has built.

The best gifts for men who have everything also carry a story. Write a short note about why you chose it. That note often matters as much as the piece.

Best gifts for 50th birthday milestones

A 50th is the birthday where a traditional gift feels right. The best gifts for 50th birthday celebrations are pieces that last long enough to be handed down, and a watch is the obvious choice. A gold dress watch or a day-date watch marks the year without needing any explanation.

If the family is pooling for the gift, choose together and keep it a surprise. Engraving a date on the caseback is a tradition some families like, but check first whether the watch allows it.

Best gifts for father in law

A father in law needs a gift that is respectful, considered and a little more formal than one for your own father. The best gifts for father in law are classic rather than playful. A gold dress watch, a structured leather folio or a plain leather belt all fit.

Ask your partner what he already wears and what he has mentioned wanting. That one conversation removes most of the guesswork. When choosing the best gifts for father in law, avoid anything that feels too familiar, like clothing in a guessed size.

A Diwali gift for father

Diwali is a natural time to thank him. In 2026, Dhanteras falls on Friday, 6 November and Diwali on Sunday, 8 November. Many families buy something lasting around Dhanteras, so plan ahead.

A Diwali gift for father works best when it feels festive but useful. A gold watch suits the occasion. A new pair of sneakers or a leather belt is a lighter gesture.
