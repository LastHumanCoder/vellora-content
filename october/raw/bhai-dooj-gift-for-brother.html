A note on the tilak

On Bhai Dooj, sisters traditionally apply a tilak on their brother's forehead, perform an aarti and pray for his long life and well-being. Brothers in turn offer a gift and a promise of care. Many families share a meal or sweets afterward. The day is often linked to the story of Yama visiting his sister Yamuna, who welcomed him with a tilak and food.

The festival has other names too: Bhau Beej in Maharashtra, Bhai Phonta in Bengal and Bhai Tika in Nepal. Customs differ from region to region and from family to family, so follow the way your own home marks the day.

Frequently asked questions

When is Bhai Dooj in 2026?
Bhai Dooj falls two days after Diwali, which is on Sunday 8 November 2026. Calendars can differ by region, so confirm the date for your city with a local panchang.
What is the tilak ceremony on Bhai Dooj?
The sister applies a tilak on her brother's forehead, performs an aarti and prays for his long life and well-being. Families then usually share a meal or sweets. Details vary by region and household.
Who gives the gift on Bhai Dooj?
Traditionally the brother gives his sister a gift. Today many sisters also give their brothers something, and in some families gifts reach the wider household too.
What is a good Bhai Dooj gift for a brother who lives in another city?
A watch or a small leather piece travels well and feels personal. Order early so it reaches him in time, and call him on the day if you cannot be there for the tilak.
